The Road to the Showdown

Five years after the first official Blood Bowl Sevens season, the community is growing and the time is right for a new challenge: a Dutch Sevens championship.

Coaches earn qualification points at recognized qualifying tournaments across the Netherlands. After one season, the top 8 meet in the Dutch Super Sevens Showdown to play for the title of Dutch Blood Bowl Sevens Champion.

How to Qualify

Play at recognized qualifiers between February 2027 and February 2028. The top 8 coaches on the ranking go to the final.

Play in 3 different locations

  • You can only qualify if you have played qualifiers in three or more different locations
  • The qualifiers are spread over seven cities, so there is usually one within reach

One result per organizer

  • If an organizer runs several qualifiers, only your best result at that organizer counts

Your best 3 count

  • Your three best results overall make up your qualification score
  • The top 8 on the ranking go to the Showdown

A NAF membership

  • Qualifiers are NAF sanctioned, so your results count toward the NAF ranking as well
  • The membership does not need to be active

Player FAQ

Quick answers to the usual questions.

Can I come along if I haven't qualified?

Yes. The championship itself is for the top 8, but alongside it there is a separate, regular Sevens tournament open to everyone. Every participant gets a Showdown goodie.

How many qualifiers do I need to play?

At least three, in three different locations. Per organizer only your best score counts, and your three best scores together make up your qualification score. You can play more, but only those three count.

Which version of Blood Bowl is played?

Blood Bowl 2025, with the Sevens rules from Spike! Magazine #22 and the official errata.

Do all qualifiers use exactly the same rules?

They all follow the same ruleset, and the DBBA publishes a Recommended Rulespack. Organizers add their own details and may deviate in places, so read the rulespack of each tournament before you register.

How can I tell if a tournament is part of the series?

Recognized qualifiers are listed on the 2027 calendar and promoted on this website.

Where and when is the Showdown?

March 2028. The location rotates through the four DBBA regions (North, East, West, South) and the date is announced three months ahead. The 2028 location is still to be announced.

Does the series also cover Elevens?

No, the Dutch Super Sevens Showdown is a Sevens championship.

Host a Qualifier

Make your tournament part of the road to the Showdown. You can, provided you meet some minimal requirements, and the DBBA will help you get there.

Why join

More coaches

  • Points on the line give coaches a reason to travel to your event

On the national map

  • Your tournament is listed and promoted on the DBBA website

Requirements

  • Contact the DBBA to join the series
  • Announce your date three months ahead
  • NAF approval — see How to NAF Approval
  • Follow the rules: Blood Bowl 2025, Spike! Magazine #22 and the errata
  • A digital rulespack — see what to include or start from the Recommended Rulespack
  • Tourplay (or equivalent) for a publicly accessible final score — see How to Tourplay

Where to start

Let the DBBA know you would like to run a qualifier. For everything about setting up the tournament itself — the checklist, rulespack, tiering, Tourplay and NAF approval — use the Tournament Organizer's Guide.

Organizer FAQ

Quick answers for tournament organizers.

How do I get my tournament recognized as a qualifier?

Let the DBBA know you would like to run a qualifier and meet the requirements. The DBBA will help you get there.

Do I have to use the DBBA Recommended Rulespack?

You need a digital rulespack that follows Blood Bowl 2025, Spike! Magazine #22 and the errata. The Recommended Rulespack is a ready-made starting point, and the TO Guide lists what a rulespack should cover.

Is NAF approval mandatory?

Yes, it is one of the requirements. See How to NAF Approval.

How will my tournament be promoted?

It is listed and promoted on the DBBA website. See the TO Guide checklist for other places to advertise.

Can I organize an Elevens qualifier?

No, the Showdown series is Sevens only.

Get in Touch

Questions, or want to run a qualifier? Reach the DBBA through the DBBA Discord, where Sevens and Elevens have separate tournament channels.
