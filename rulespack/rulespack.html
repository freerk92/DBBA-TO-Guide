DBBA Recommended Rulespack
These are the DBBA's recommended rules for Blood Bowl Sevens tournaments. Tournament organisers add their own schedule, prizes, venue and entry details.

01Ruleset

  • Blood Bowl 2025 rules, with the Sevens rules from Blood Bowl Spike! Magazine #22, and official errata.
  • The tournament is NAF sanctioned.

02Format

  • Resurrection: every match starts with the team as registered. No SPP or injuries carry over between matches.
  • Swiss pairing: winners play winners and losers play losers. Rematches are avoided where possible.

03Team building

  • Team value: up to 600,000 gold pieces.
  • Permitted teams: the teams listed in the tier table.
  • Star Players: not allowed.
  • Inducements: bought at team creation from your 600,000 gold, and kept for the whole tournament. Tourplay shows which inducements are available to your team.

04Improvement pack

Casualties count when you cause them on your opponent's players: blocks, fouls, crowd pushes, Secret Weapons, and thrown or kicked players.

07Tiebreakers

When coaches finish on equal points, these decide the ranking, in order:

  1. Score in direct matches
  2. Touchdown difference in direct matches
  3. Touchdown difference
  4. Casualty difference in direct matches
  5. Casualty difference
  6. Team value (lowest wins)

This is the default tiebreaker order in Tourplay.
